# Rome 1987: period facts for *Il quarto nome*

Date: 2026-09-29. Research for [#16](https://github.com/Imbustai/imbustai-app/issues/16) on the wayfinder map [#9](https://github.com/Imbustai/imbustai-app/issues/9). This is a fact sheet the case file can cite. It is not an approved story change: the "Use in the story" notes are suggestions for the Story's authors.

Terms follow `packages/story-engine/CONTEXT.md`. The **Lead** is Commissario Giacomo Lombardo. Voss, Adelaide and the Pubblico Ministero (PM) are **Characters**. A telegram is a **Dispatch**, and the PM's closing letter is the **Epilogue**. Facts the mystery depends on are **Plot keys**; invented, non-decisive detail is **Texture**.

## The findings that constrain the case file most

1. **An agent could not run a line-up.** Under the 1930 code, the formal *ricognizione di persona* was an act of the judge, repeated separately for each sworn witness, and defence counsel had the right to attend ([art. 360][cpp-360], [362][cpp-362], [363][cpp-363], [304-bis][cpp-304bis]). From November 1974 the police could run an urgent identification themselves, under three conditions: it had to be an *ufficiale* of judicial police, the prosecutor had to be told first, and the suspect's lawyer had to be present ([art. 225][cpp-225], as replaced by [Legge 497/1974][l497-7]). In the late 1970s a *guardia* like the young Voss could set a line-up up but could not lawfully conduct one. If he "ran" it, it was an informal, unrecorded identification, which is exactly the kind of act that would haunt him. See §2.
2. **In the late 1970s Voss was a "guardia", not an "agente".** The Polizia di Stato and the rank "agente" date from the 1981 reform ([L.121/1981][l121-23]); [DPR 336/1982][d336-18] turned *guardie* into *agenti*. An agent with five years' service could be promoted *agente scelto* on merit ([DPR 335/1982 art. 7][d335-7]), so a veteran still plain "Agente" in 1987 implies a stalled career. See §1.
3. **A family certificate issued in 1987 cannot show Luca.** Until June 1989 a *certificato di stato di famiglia* had to show the household "all'atto del rilascio", on the day it was issued ([DPR 136/1958 art. 30][d136-30]). Certificates of "situazioni anagrafiche pregresse" (past situations) only became a right with [DPR 223/1989 art. 35][d223-35], in force from 23 June 1989. The document that names the stepfather should be one of two things:
   - a 1977–79 certificate, for example one kept in the trial file; or
   - a written attestation drawn from the archived family card ([art. 24][d136-24]).

   Calling it a "certificato storico" in 1987 is a two-year anachronism. There is also a bonus clue. Anyone who asked for certificates about another household had to show ID, and the clerk had to transcribe it ([art. 29][d136-29]). A killer tracing the witnesses' addresses would leave a paper trail. See §4.
4. **Luca's death record hides both the prison and the stepfather.** A death in prison, or a violent death, was not to be mentioned in the death act, and the act names the father and mother, not a stepfather ([R.D. 1238/1939 art. 140][sc-140]). See §3.
5. **"Convicted" needs a Plot key decision.** It matters whether Luca's conviction was final (irrevocable) when he died:
   - If it was not yet final, his death extinguished the offence ([c.p. art. 150][cp-150]) and no retrial for review (*revisione*) exists, because revisione covers only irrevocable convictions ([c.p.p. 1930 art. 553][cpp-553]).
   - If it was final, a close relative could still ask for revisione after his death ([art. 556][cpp-556]). A stepfather counts as a relative by marriage (*affine*) under [c.p. art. 307][cp-307] and [c.c. art. 78][cc-78].

   See §2.
6. **Letters were slow and cheap; telegrams were fast.** The postal administration's own 1987 average delivery time was "più di tre giorni e mezzo", and a Confindustria test found more than five and a half days ([Repubblica, 23 Jul 1987][rep-19870723]). An official 1988 survey found that a letter took 7.8 days from Milan to Rome and 19.9 days from Milan to Cagliari ([Repubblica, 4 Sep 1988][rep-19880904]). A Letter from Rome to a small island should therefore take a week or more; a telegram arrives the same day. A letter cost 600 lire, and an ordinary telegram 2,800 lire for up to ten words ([GU, 15 Nov 1986][gu-p12]). See §6.
7. **Panorama came out on Mondays.** In 1987 it was "in edicola" on Monday, like L'Espresso ([Repubblica, 13 Sep 1987][rep-19870913]). A copy stolen "every Thursday" works if Adelaide is a subscriber whose copy comes by post; otherwise move the theft to Monday. See §8.
8. **Via Lanciani is not in Testaccio.** Via Rodolfo Lanciani is in the Nomentano quarter (Municipio II, CAP 00162), near Piazza Bologna ([OpenStreetMap][osm-lanciani]). The Subplot must pick one of the two places. See §10.
9. **Baudo was 1987's biggest TV gossip.** He hosted Sanremo in February 1987, clashed with RAI's president over the word "nazionalpopolare", moved to Berlusconi's Fininvest, and from September 1987 fronted *Festival* on Canale 5 ([Treccani][tr-baudo], [Mediaset][tgcom-festival]). See §9.
10. **The Epilogue's legal vocabulary depends on its date.** The 1930 code applied until 24 October 1989 ([DPR 447/1988][d447-1]). Under it, the operative part of the verdict (the *dispositivo*) is read out in open court straight away ([art. 472][cpp-472]). The full reasoned sentence was due within 15 days ([art. 151][cpp-151]). A quick ending is plausible if the stepfather confesses, because a confession sends the case to the faster, prosecutor-led *istruzione sommaria* ([art. 389][cpp-389]). See §7.

## How to read this sheet

- **Confidence** has three levels:
  - **High**: read in a primary source. That means the law as in force on the relevant date (Normattiva point-in-time versions, or the historical versions of the 1930 code in the Gazzetta Ufficiale), an official page, or a contemporary newspaper reporting official figures.
  - **Medium**: a reliable secondary source, or primary evidence that is close but not exact (for example, a 1989 source for a 1987 fact).
  - **Low**: an inference or a weak anchor.
- Laws are linked as they stood in the year that matters. The `!vig=` date in a Normattiva link selects the version in force on that date.
- Method limits:
  - Web search and fetch hit a usage limit partway through, so some later checks were made with direct downloads of known URLs.
  - The La Stampa archive search could not be scripted, so contemporary press comes from La Repubblica's archive, which has full text from 1984.

## 2. The late-1970s line-up and the Corte d'Assise trial
